fix(cn): retire a failed query's parked output instead of wiping every query's

An engine Err used to clear every parked sender output on the CN and poison
every slot with that query's error, and nothing else ever released a dead
query's output: q16's translation failure carried 6.6 GB into q17, and a failed
query's queued senders kept running (up to 38 GB parked under later queries on
the 1-CN SF1000 sweep). Parked output is now owned per query (ParkedRegistry);
a query is retired by its own Err or by the CN's fail_fragment, and its runs
are refused at the engine dequeue (inside run_fragment_inner so the lease sweep
still runs), at park time, on the dispatch worker and on arrival; a
StagedLeases guard returns the staged leases of a fragment that never runs.
Tests: 9 registry, 5 service (both dispatch modes), 4 engine (GPU); CI trio
green. Left: cancel_plan_fragment teardown and rendezvous purge (next commit).
