## Open items carried from the stages
- Stream-hop placements carry `pipeline_uuid = nil` at registration (the receiver's pipelines do not exist at relay/push time); the claiming task adopts them in the in-memory registry only, so the analyzer's per-operator filters do not see them yet.
- `scan_split_read` brackets `materialize_table`, which fuses datasource I/O and cudf decode; a per-byte-range issue/complete needs datasource-level counters (`src/io/sirius_datasource.cpp`) or a new entity — a decision for the maintainers, not taken here.
- Cost: with a telemetry context present the scan probe builds the `sources` string per split and the arena probe allocates a DataBatch handle per lease even when `enable_quent: false` selects the no-op exporter; keep both off in wall-clock YAMLs (they were on in every timing run of the sibling analysis, on both arms).
- Flush on shutdown (found by agent 8 while replaying CN sessions): the ndjson exporter's per-type buffers flush independently and the final flush is lost when the CN exits on SIGTERM (`experimental/starrocks/src/main.rs:673-681` shutdown path), so a warm session keeps only a 14–15-fragment prefix and CNs that never hosted a merge lose their `operator` declarations. A capture that must be complete has to drain the exporter before the engine tears down (or the analyzer must tolerate a task-only session, as `scratchpad/sim8/repair.py` does). Not fixed in this change.
