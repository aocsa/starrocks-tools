# Handoff: StarRocks-on-Sirius profiling and fixes, as of 2026-09-03 18:30 UTC

Written one hour before the GB200 box went down for maintenance (2026-09-03 19:00 UTC to 2026-09-14). Everything
referenced here is in this repo (`aocsa/starrocks-tools`) or on branches of `aocsa/sirius`; nothing is on the box
that is not also here. Read this, then `docs/reports/sf1000-planning-cardinality-backpressure.md`, then `fix/INTEGRATION.md`.

## 1. What was solved (measured, published)

**Carve check (demo).** `demo/q1q6-integration` = `origin/dev` 98fe1a84 + the 12 open Draft PRs + 10 carved commits
reproduces the multi-CN system: TPC-H q01/q06 match the DuckDB oracle at SF1 and SF10 on 4 CNs (one per GPU), warm and
after cold restarts; CI-equivalent checks green. Draft PR aocsa/sirius#3 (do not merge). Cherry-pick plan per carved
commit in `docs/plans/demo-q1q6-report.md`.

**Perf campaign SF10/SF100/SF1000 (decimal data).** Two measured causes of the CN-path gap, both fixed behind flags on
`perf/float-sum-canonicalize-flag`:
- q01 was 4.9x slower than standalone at SF1000 because the CN path lowers decimal sums to FP64 and the aggregate then
  sorts every batch (`canonicalize_row_order`); gated off by `SIRIUS_CANONICAL_FLOAT_SUMS` (default off): 25.75 s -> 4.95 s at 1 CN.
- q06 did not scale because the sender fragment ran inside the `exec_plan_fragment` RPC while the FE waits for the first
  wave; opt-in `SIRIUS_CN_ASYNC_SENDER_DISPATCH=1` dispatches sender-only fragments off the RPC thread: q06 4 CN 1240 -> 844 ms.
Baselines and A/Bs: `docs/plans/starrocks-sirius-perf-plan.md`.

**Quent instrumentation** (`perf/quent-instrument`): scan split reads, stream hops (relayed/pushed), staging-arena leases,
CN fragment labels `<query id>:<fragment instance id>`, explicit `enable_quent` in the derived CN YAML (off unless
`SIRIUS_CN_ENABLE_QUENT=1`). Report: `docs/reports/quent-instrument-report.md`.

**SF1000 profiling, planning / cardinality / backpressure** (`docs/reports/sf1000-planning-cardinality-backpressure.md`,
33-agent workflow, 9 findings double-confirmed). Headlines:
- 15 of 22 queries run on the CN path at SF1000 (1 and 4 CNs); q05 q08 q09 q17 q18 q21 die after 54-232 s in the engine's
  100-retry OOM loop; standalone Sirius runs all six in 2.7-6.5 s with exact answers; q16 is a translator gap.
- The FE plans every FILES() scan at 1 row (`StatisticsCalculator.computeFileScanNode`), so join distribution is data-blind
  and flips with the alive-node count (1 CN: broadcast, incl. 3.79B lineitem rows as a semi-join build; 4 CNs: shuffle the fact table).
- Nothing streams across fragments: every exchange is a full GPU materialisation; parked repositories are invisible to the
  downgrade sweep (cannot spill); the reschedule loop has no feasibility check; a failed query's remaining senders still park
  output. Shuffle drains are serialised; concurrent scans on 4 GPUs cost 1.3-1.6x per task.
- Correctness: q15 returns 0 rows in most CN runs (two independently computed FP64 sums compared for equality; the
  canonical-sort gate does not fix it); the `1 - 0.07` FP64->DECIMAL truncation affects q01 q03 q07 q10 q15 q19 (on dev via #1236).

**Fixes designed and implemented (branches on aocsa/sirius, specs in `fix/`).**
- Fix 1 `fix/oom-failfast` (19f9eee4): the executor fails the query when an OOM retry cannot make progress (freed 0 bytes,
  pool at its limit, no completion since the previous attempt), with an error naming the bytes held outside task
  reservations; Catch2 tests; reviewed (minor findings only) and independently verified (build, tests, lint). Predicted:
  the six failures die in ~3 s instead of 54-232 s. NOT yet measured at SF1000 (arm V1a/V1b).
- Fix 2 `fix/parked-bookkeeping` (63e7e0c1, 0f4b1c19): per-query retirement of parked sender outputs, skip of a retired
  query's pending fragments, `cancel_plan_fragment` tears the query down on the CN; Rust tests; reviewed and verified.
  Predicted: removes the 0-38 GB leaks between queries and the stolen engine time. NOT yet measured (arms V2a-c).
- Fix 3 `fix/files-cardinality`: FE patch (bytes/width estimate on `TableFunctionTable`, mutable Config knob, default on)
  plus proto/CN exact `num_rows` from parquet footers; committed as 7f38171c (FE patch, JUnit tests 2/2) and 00302c5c
  (CN returns the footer row total with the schema RPC), pushed to aocsa/sirius; an earlier uncommitted snapshot is in
  `fix/wip-files-cardinality/`. Review/verify were still running at shutdown; NOT built end to end with an FE rebuild by the
  orchestrator; Gate A (EXPLAIN COSTS at 1 and 4 CNs) NOT run.
- Fix 4 `fix/fragment-fusion`: spec only (`fix/fragment-fusion-SPEC.md`): fuse a single-destination local leaf sender into
  its receiver plan on 1 CN (`leaf` mode), predicted 5/6 (q21 needs `all` mode or RIGHT_SEMI); plus plan item 5, the
  translator RIGHT_SEMI arm. NOT started (worktree existed, no commits).

## 3. Paths still to explore (ranked by the report's section 4, not yet attempted)

- Streaming receivers (build side first, probe streamed) as the long-term fix for "every exchange is a full GPU
  materialisation"; touches the engine's single-flight lifecycle (`sirius_context.cpp` begin_execution_window).
- Spillable parked repositories (register streaming-sink outputs and receiver inputs with the downgrade sweep; HOST-tier
  `output_row_count`/`export_packed`): the alternative or complement to fusion for q09/q21-sized intermediates.
- Remote runtime filters mapped onto the engine's dynamic-filter channel (q02 min-subquery, q20, q07).
- Move `export_packed` off the engine thread; concurrent per-destination drains (0.15-0.3 s per shuffle stage at 4 CNs).
- Cut the fixed ~200 ms FE head (8 serial FILES() schema RPCs per query; TCP_NODELAY on CN sockets).
- Staging arena backpressure instead of hard failure at ~87% fill (q03/q07 at 4 CNs).
- Cold-start scan reservation 8x over-estimate; join/receiver fragments over-reserve 4-6x (mechanism not investigated).
- Why per-task GPU_SCAN time rises 1.3-1.6x when four CNs scan concurrently (read vs decode not separable yet).
- Whether the six failures pass at 4 CNs (never tried), and warm medians with n > 2.
