## 3. Findings by dimension

Notation: **P** planning, **C** cardinality, **B** backpressure. Confirmed findings first in each dimension (both a data skeptic and a source skeptic failed to refute them; their corrections are folded in), then disputed findings with the objection stated, then lower-ranked findings that were not adversarially verified (marked as such).

### 3A. Planning

#### P1 (confirmed). The six 1-CN failures are one FE plan shape: an unfiltered lineitem leaf feeding a hash-partitioned exchange, whose whole output must be parked on the GPU

**Measured.** In all six plans the failing fragment is a bare lineitem `FileScanNode` under a `HASH_PARTITIONED` `TDataStreamSink` with one destination (survey/plan-summary.txt lines 10, 16, 18, 34, 36, 42; dumps cn1/dump/fragment-0141 (q05, node 8), 0208 (q08), 0220 (q09), 0427 (q17, node 0), 0437 (q18, node 9), 0504 (q21, node 12), each `output_partition TPartitionType(2)` = HASH_PARTITIONED per `Partitions.thrift:41-49`). The FE join labels differ (q05/q08/q09/q17 PARTITIONED, q18 `11:INNER JOIN (BUCKET_SHUFFLE(S))`, q21 `14:LEFT SEMI JOIN (BUCKET_SHUFFLE(S))`), the sink type does not. Lineitem is the build-side exchange in five plans and the probe-side exchange in q17. Every one of the six failing fragments was started as `role=sender inputs=0 outputs=1` (cn1/cluster.log lines 4200, 7854, 9840, 16770, 18095, 20282).

Because nothing streams across a fragment boundary (finding B1), the sender must park its entire projection. Size of that projection (standalone `GPU_SCAN in=` of the same scan, agent-notes/standalone-failed-quent.txt) versus what fit before the pool filled (cn1-quent.txt `STREAMING_SINK in=`, engine-cn0.log `[gpu_pool] QueryBegin/QueryEnd allocated=`):

| query | lineitem projection (GB) | pool before the sender started (GB) | parked by the sender at death (GB) | pool at death (GB) | stuck tasks x 100 retries |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| q05 | 171.00 | 0.00 | 104.30 | 104.30 | 25 |
| q08 | 195.75 | 4.70 (q05 leftovers) | 99.54 | 104.24 | 36 |
| q09 | 244.50 | 8.83 (q08 leftovers) | 96.42 | 105.24 | 56 |
| q17 | 122.25 | 38.16 (q09 + q16 leftovers) | 66.26 | 104.42 | 21 |
| q18 | 97.50 | 68.69 (own senders: customer 3.94 + agg 24.0 + orders 36.75, plus 4.0 q17 leftover) | 35.87 | 104.56 | 23 |
| q21 | 73.50 (the 28-task l2 leaf) | 77.76 (own filtered l1/l3 sender) | 25.37 | 103.14 | 18 |

Pool cap = 107,374,182,400 bytes (100 GiB); every one of the 17,917 OOM lines in cn1/engine-cn0.log reads `global usage 107374182400 bytes`. `begin + parked = end` holds within 0.01 GB in every row.

Correction to the original claim: "the sender cannot fit its own output" is true for q05/q08/q09/q17 (projection > 107 GB alone) but not for q18 (97.5 GB) or q21 (73.5 GB), which died because the query's other exchange inputs were parked at the same time. The accurate statement is: the sum of concurrently parked exchange inputs of one query (plus leftovers of earlier failed queries, finding B4) exceeds the pool, and nothing can be spilled (finding B2). Per-row widths in the engine are 12-41 B (decimals arrive as 8-byte values), so the analyst's "168-456 GB" range with decimal128 widths overstates three of the six.

**Measured, standalone contrast.** The same SQL on the same GPU and pool passes because DuckDB's stats-aware plan streams lineitem as the probe of a hash join built on the small filtered dimension chain: standalone-failed/runs/q05.explain.txt:50-61 has `READ_PARQUET lineitem ~6,110,827,680 rows` as the probe child with a ~61.6M-row build; q18.explain.txt:37-56 likewise; q17/q21 use DELIM joins. standalone-failed-quent q17 `HASH_JOIN(18) n=46 in=120.54 GB` shows lineitem probing in 46 batches, never materialised. Standalone still materialises lineitem-derived aggregate state (q18 PARTITION(5) 36.19 GB), just not the raw projection.

**Root cause.** `StatisticsCalculator.computeFileScanNode` (FE `sql/optimizer/statistics/StatisticsCalculator.java:664-676`): `// cause we don't know the real schema in file, just use the default Row Count now` `builder.setOutputRowCount(1)`, every column `ColumnStatistic.unknown()`. card-compare-cn1.txt shows FE estimate 1 for every exchange of the six queries against actuals of 150,000,000 / 227,571,151 (q05 s1/s4) and 730,806,711 (q21 s6). With every side at 1 row the CBO's fragment cut is arithmetic on column widths and node count (finding P3).

**Not measured.** Whether the six pass on 4 CNs. cn4/runs/runs.csv has no rows for them. A hash-partitioned exchange splits parked bytes across CNs (cn4 q03.r1: the 3.23B-row lineitem shuffle parked 19.60/19.67/19.71/19.86 GB per CN), so the per-CN lineitem share would be ~31-61 GB and the expected outcome is pass (inference). The 4-CN plans are also not identical: q21's `10:RIGHT ANTI JOIN` becomes `BUCKET_SHUFFLE(S)` with a local build (plan-summary-4cn.txt line 42).

**Proposed changes.**

1. FE: real FILES() cardinalities (finding C1, section 4 item 3). Caveat from the source skeptic (inference, medium confidence): with real statistics the small side of lineitem's join is orders-derived (q18 ~308M rows, q05 227M rows, tens of GB), `CostModel.java:428` multiplies a broadcast whose build exceeds `exec_mem_limit` (2 GiB, `SessionVariable.java:1305`) by `BROADCAST_JOIN_MEM_EXCEED_PENALTY` = 1000, and the shuffle network cost is 0 on a single node (`CostModel.java:437-440`). Real row counts alone may therefore still put lineitem across an exchange on 1 CN; this needs an `EXPLAIN COSTS` with statistics, which was not run.
2. CN, 1-CN only: fuse a leaf scan fragment into its single-destination receiver before translation, at the `TPlan` level (replace the receiver's `EXCHANGE_NODE` with the sender's node list; `ExchangeNode.java:183/360` guarantee the row layout matches). On 1 CN every exchange is local, so the fused plan is the standalone plan that passes all six. Substantial work; medium confidence. Caveat: for q21 the fused lineitem would sit on the build side of a `LEFT SEMI` that the translator cannot flip (no `RIGHT_SEMI`, finding P5), so q21 needs P5 as well.
3. Engine: fail fast (finding B3) so a wrong plan costs ~3 s instead of 54-232 s.

Confidence: high on the diagnosis; medium on which fix path is accepted.

#### P2 (confirmed). q02: the FE decorrelates the min-subquery into a full 800M-row partsupp chain and shuffles a 1-row region; its runtime filters are dropped by the CN

**Measured, 1 CN.** card-compare-cn1.txt lines 4-15: exch 3 BROADCAST 800,000,000 rows (partsupp as the build of join 4); exch 28 SHUFFLE 800,000,000 by `n_regionkey` into join 31 PARTITIONED against exch 30 = 1 row (region EUROPE); exch 34/36 = 118,065,576-row min table. survey/explain/q02.costs.txt:305-327 (join 31), 519-535 (join 4), 73-97 (join 37 `build runtime filters filter_id=7 build_expr=ps_partkey remote=true`).

The engine rescues the build side inside F00: cn1-quent.txt:118-122 fragment 56c `DYNAMIC_FILTER(4) n=5 in=13.10GB` -> 0.05 GB, `HASH_JOIN(7)` input 0.07 GB (DuckDB inside the fragment built on the filtered part and join-filtered the parked partsupp stream). The 13.1 GB partsupp stream was still scanned and parked in full (fragment 56d GPU_SCAN 0.788 s). The cost that is not rescued is the subquery chain: fragment 566 (F11, partsupp x supplier x nation) `GPU_SCAN(6) 0.779 + HASH_JOIN(10) 0.289 + HASH_JOIN(14) 0.399 s`, wall 438 ms of a 1.063 s span (41%), plus F19 `HASH_JOIN(7) 0.243 s` over 13.0 GB and the min aggregation. Standalone (standalone-quent.txt:25-30) spends 1.826 s Computing of which 1.769 s is GPU_SCAN (it also scans partsupp twice) and 0.057 s everything else; its `LEFT_DELIM_JOIN` (standalone/runs/q02.explain.txt:52, 142-157) joins the subquery's partsupp with `DELIM_SCAN` (the outer's distinct p_partkeys) before the min aggregation. cn1 Computing 2.9 s vs standalone 1.8 s (reservation-ratio.txt lines 4, 34): the ~1.0 s excess is joins and aggregates over 800M rows.

Runtime filters are dropped, measured: the FE attached filter 6 (`r_regionkey` -> F14 nation scan) and shipped `TRuntimeFilterDescription` entries with `has_remote_targets: true` (cn1/dump/fragment-0025.txt:1074-1275), yet exch 25 delivered all 25 nations (card-compare-cn1.txt, q02 exch 25 row), so exch 28 carried 800M rows instead of ~160M. `grep -rn runtime_filter` over `crates/starrocks-plan-translator/src` and `experimental/starrocks/src` finds only a test fixture (wire_type_parity.rs:607).

**Measured, 4 CN.** The plan changes (13 -> 16 fragments): exch 4, 21, 26, 31 = 800,000,000 rows each (card-compare-cn4.txt lines 4-18; two are raw partsupp scans, two are re-shuffles of the joined 800M-row intermediate on `s_nationkey` and `n_regionkey`), exch 39 BROADCAST = 118,065,576 rows delivered to 4 CNs (card_compare sums receivers, hence "472M"). 135 nixl transmits, 43.6-45.7 GB per run (cn4-transmits.txt). The `n_regionkey` shuffle (5 distinct values) is skewed: cn4-cnlog.json q02.r0 stream 31 per receiver = 479,981,680 / 160,046,880 / 159,971,440 / 0 rows. Total Computing went from 2.9 s to 4.6 s over 4 CNs = 1.15 s per CN, but the span did not move (1.063 -> 1.077 s), Queued = 0: q02 on 4 CNs is wait-dominated.

Two further q02 facts: ~200 ms of the 1-CN vs standalone gap is outside the engine span (finding P4), and on 4 CNs one of the two partsupp scan fragments (c472-c475) ran at 0.21 s/GB while the other (c464-c467) ran at 0.05 s/GB in the same run; that one fragment is 348 ms = 32% of the span and tracks the run-to-run spread (MFS-5, unverified, cause unknown).

**Proposed changes.** (1) Real FILES() cardinalities (C1) so nation/region are broadcast and never the shuffled side; note this does not by itself recreate the DELIM shape, which needs (2) remote runtime filters implemented in the translator/CN, mapping `THashJoinNode.build_runtime_filters` / `TPlanNode.probe_runtime_filters` onto the engine's dynamic-filter channel (`docs/super-sirius/dynamic-filters.md:58-94`); the FE already pushes them through aggregates (`AggregationNode.pushDownRuntimeFilters :448-464`). Confidence high on the diagnosis, low on the effort of (2). A CN->FE "re-plan" request does not exist and is not a feasible interim.

**Mechanism, corrected by the source skeptic.** The cited term `CostModel.visitPhysicalDistribution` BROADCAST = `outputSize * aliveBackendNumber` (`CostModel.java:419-424`) is inert in this deployment: the Sirius CNs register as COMPUTE NODEs (`experimental/starrocks/src/lib.rs:210-212` `ALTER SYSTEM ADD COMPUTE NODE`; cn4/cluster.log:201 `run_mode is shared_data`), and `ctx.getAliveBackendNumber()` counts only BACKENDs (`system/SystemInfoService.java:1047`), so it is 0. The node count enters through `HashJoinCostModel.getMemCost` (`sql/optimizer/cost/HashJoinCostModel.java:115-127`: `beNum = max(1, aliveBackendNumber + aliveComputeNodeNumber)` in shared-data mode, `memCost = rightOutput * beNum` for BROADCAST, weight 2 in `CostModel.getRealCost`). With every child at 1 row, `Statistics.getOutputSize` is the sum of projected type widths, and the rule reduces to: broadcast the right side iff its row width is below roughly the left width at 1 CN, and below roughly a quarter of it at 4 CNs. This reproduces every observed decision (q03: build 16 B vs probe 24 B -> BROADCAST at 1 CN, PARTITIONED at 4; customer 4 B vs orders 20 B -> BROADCAST at both; the survivors q22 `o_custkey` 4 B, q07 `c_custkey+c_nationkey`, q02 `ps_partkey+min` are the narrow ones). `checkBroadcastRowCountLimit` (`task/EnforceAndCostTask.java:281-325`) never fires because it needs `rightChildStats.getOutputRowCount() > 15,000,000` (`SessionVariable.java:1833`).

**What a row-count-only fix would and would not do (inference, hand computation of the FE cost code, medium confidence).** Giving FILES() scans a row count but leaving column stats UNKNOWN makes the FE estimate a join output as `max(L, R)` and range predicates at 0.5, so orders x customer in q03 is estimated at 375M-750M rows. That *arms* `checkBroadcastRowCountLimit` against the broadcast the finding wants: broadcast is rejected when the right side is > 15M rows and the left is less than 10x its bytes (`EnforceAndCostTask.java:324-325`). Result: q12 and q19 flip to BROADCAST (saving ~26 GB), q03 and q10's customer join stay PARTITIONED, q14 stays a wash. Getting the q03/q10 plans additionally requires raising `broadcast_row_limit` / `broadcast_right_table_scale_factor` (`SessionVariable.java:1833-1836`), or column statistics (NDV) for FILES(), or neutralising the probe-cache penalty in `HashJoinCostModel` (`BROADCAST_MAT_RATIO` 12 vs `SHUFFLE_MAX_RATIO` 3).

**Existing levers.** SQL join hints `JOIN [BROADCAST|SHUFFLE]` are honoured (`sql/parser/AstBuilder.java:6567-6575`, `JoinHelper.java:344-349`), but the grammar accepts them only in explicit `JOIN ... ON` syntax, not the kit's comma joins, and a hint disables reorder/commutativity for that join. `broadcast_row_limit = 0` forces shuffle everywhere (`EnforceAndCostTask.java:312-314`), which would help q22/q07/q02 and hurt q03/q12/q19. `cbo_debug_alive_backend_number` (`SessionVariable.java:1823`) changes only the BE count and cannot reproduce the 1-CN plan at 4 CNs.

Confidence: high on the flip and the byte inflation; medium on the corrected cost arithmetic (not executed in the FE).

Confidence high on the mechanism; medium (1) and low-medium (2) on design effort.

## 5. Open questions and what was not measured

1. **4-CN behaviour of q05 q08 q09 q17 q18 q21.** Never run (cn4/runs/runs.csv). The per-CN parked lineitem share would be ~31-61 GB, so passing is expected, but q09's per-CN share (61 GB) plus its other shuffled streams is untested, and the 4-CN plans differ in shape (q21).
2. **FE plans with real statistics.** No `EXPLAIN COSTS` was run with FILES() row counts or column statistics; the predicted outcomes in P1/P3 (broadcast penalty, guard, width rule) are hand computations of the cost code. The hint experiments (P3) were not run.
3. **Cause of the 1.3-1.6x per-task scan slowdown at 4 CNs and the q01 0.5-0.87 s split stalls** (B7). The probe cannot separate file read from decode and the digest dropped its bytes. Also open: the q02 4-CN partsupp fragment that reads at 0.21 s/GB while its sibling reads at 0.05 s/GB in the same run (MFS-5).
4. **Whether the deploy-level gaps are Nagle/delayed ACK** (P4): inferred from the pattern (gaps only after multi-reply levels) and the absence of `set_nodelay` on accepted sockets; a one-line test exists.
5. **q10's oracle mismatch**: resolved (section 1): same 20 customers, revenue sums low by up to 3.7e-3, five rows reordered; no remaining open question beyond the decimal lowering itself (section 4, item 9).
6. **q11 returns 0 rows on both engines** because the bench kit does not scale the 0.0001 fraction; the compare cannot confirm correctness of q11 at SF1000.
7. **cn1 timings of q10-q16 and q22.r1/r2 were taken with 31.6 / 36.6 GB of leaked parked output in the pool** (B4). Re-run on a clean CN before quoting them; the reservation-gate blocks in C3 depend on that leak.
8. **Sample size.** Warm medians are n=2; cn4 q02 has a 21% spread (1264 vs 1044 ms) that tracks one partsupp scan fragment; the six failures and their standalone counterparts ran once each.
9. **Can the engine run two fragments concurrently on one `SiriusContext`?** The single-flight `query_lifecycle_mutex_` and shared `task_creator_` say no today; the cost of lifting that (needed for streaming receivers, B1 route 2) was not scoped.
10. **Host spill throughput.** B2's "five of six would pass" assumes a working spill at 279 GB GPU+HOST; no spill bandwidth was measured, and q09 is marginal.
11. **`enable_single_node_schedule` with FILES()** qualifies by code reading (`JobSpec.supportSingleNodeParallelSchedule`, `ScanNode.isConnectorScanNode`) but was not exercised.
12. **The receive-side unpack at 2.1 TB/s vs the pack at 63 GB/s** points at the 8 MiB `chunked_pack` (B5) but no alternative pack was timed.
13. **Standalone q11 scans partsupp once** (Quent shows one 16.4 GB GPU_SCAN for two CTE_SCANs) while the plan shows a CTE with two consumers; how the second consumer is served was not pursued.
14. **Session cold start.** cn1 q01 cold 9359 ms vs 5073 warm: the first fragment's summed `GPU_SCAN` was 25.9 s vs 12.2 s (cn1-quent.txt q01.r0 / r1), i.e. the very first GPU scan of the CN process runs ~2x slower per split; standalone's first query shows a smaller version (Comp 23.0 vs 19.0 s). Not separated into GPFS/O_DIRECT first-touch, CUDA module load, or pool warm-up.
15. **Standalone warm-run spread.** standalone q03 2479/3622 ms and q04 2113/1678 ms (runs.csv); the slow q03 run is a slow scan (standalone-quent.txt lines 43-54: `GPU_SCAN(10)` 11.24 s vs 7.20 s summed). With n=2 the q03/q04 1-CN parity ratios (1.02/0.98) are not distinguishable from 1.1-1.25. Re-run with n>=5 before quoting parity for these two.
16. **Join-fragment over-reservation.** q04 F00 253 GB requested for 61 GB peak, q07 F08 163 GB for 26 GB (C3). The hash-join / partition / concat reservation estimates were not read; whether they use a no-history multiplier like the scan's 8x is unknown.
17. **q16 workaround.** `SET new_planner_agg_stage = 1` is only the CN's error-text suggestion; not run. q16 also has the same partsupp-broadcast (exch 2) and `NULL AWARE LEFT ANTI JOIN (BROADCAST)` on supplier (exch 7) shape as the other WITH queries (survey/plan-summary.txt line 32), so it will inherit P2/P5 once it translates.
18. **Shuffle skew at 4 CNs** (P3): q07 exch 20 2.7x, q02 exch 31 one empty CN, q11 exch 4 2.7x, all on low-NDV nation/region keys. No wall-clock loss was attributable at these volumes; not measured for a shuffle where the skewed stream is the large one.
